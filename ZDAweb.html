What is ZDA?

ZDA is an independent development shop building secure, zero-knowledge-powered applications on Zcash. We operate outside the Dev Fund pipeline and are accountable only to our contributors.

Why support ZDA?

  • 👤 No conflicts of interest
    Not tied to Dev Fund or their subsidiaries
  • 🛡 Privacy-first approach
    Every project uses Zcash shielded transactions
  • 🔧 Innovation-centric
    We build what no one else is prioritizing
